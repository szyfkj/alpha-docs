# Video

**Being written.**

Video is a short-video content tool: download videos from Xiaohongshu, TikTok and
Douyin, transcribe, translate and subtitle them, then cut new videos from the clips,
images and music in your media library with a prompt, and follow their views and ad
performance once they're live.

For Video operating instructions, please get in touch — this guide is still being
