# Task photos on the phone

When you carry out a **Process** or **Consolidate** task from the phone's **Warehouse
Worker** home → **Workflow Actions**, each photo section (**PHOTOS**, **PHOTOS BEFORE
PACKING** and each new box's **BOX PHOTOS (show the label)**) has two buttons, as on the
**Inbound** page:

- **Camera**: opens the rear camera full screen inside the page. Tap **Capture Photo** to
  add the picture as a thumbnail, or the close button at the top right to go back without
  one.
- **Upload**: opens the gallery or file chooser, where you can pick several photos at once.

If the camera can't be opened (for example, camera access wasn't allowed), "Unable to
access camera" shows under the buttons. You can still add photos with **Upload**.

Before, there was a single **Add photo** button, and in the Warehouse Worker app it only
offered photos from the gallery.
