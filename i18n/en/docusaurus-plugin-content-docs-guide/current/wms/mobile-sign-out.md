# Going back and signing out on the phone

Pressing back on the phone's **Warehouse Worker** home (the toolbar's back arrow, the back
gesture or the browser's back button) first opens a **Sign Out** dialog: "You will need to
sign in again to receive or move packages on this device."

- Tap **Sign out** to end the session and return to the sign-in page.
- Tap **Cancel** to close the dialog and stay on the home page. Pressing back again shows
  the dialog again.

Pressing back on a task page such as **Inbound** or **Move** still returns straight to the
home page, with no dialog.

Back on the home page no longer closes the app or drops you on the sign-in page; to leave,
tap **Sign out** in the dialog.
